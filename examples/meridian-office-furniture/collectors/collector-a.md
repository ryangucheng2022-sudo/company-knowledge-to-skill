# Collector A output — Meridian Office Furniture (FICTIONAL)

1. **QBR** — Quarterly Business Review. Held in the second week after quarter
   close; each department presents results against plan.
2. **Weekly operations meeting** — called the **MBR (Monday Business Review)**,
   every Monday 09:00, chaired by Operations.
3. **Free shipping threshold** — orders over USD 75 ship free. (I recall it used
   to be USD 50 a couple of years ago.)
4. **POD** — for white-glove deliveries the driver must take a proof-of-delivery
   photo. I don't know which team owns this rule.
